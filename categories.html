Food Categories

Fruits

Apples, bananas, oranges, and berries provide vitamins and fiber.

Vegetables

Spinach, carrots, broccoli, and tomatoes provide essential nutrients.

Whole Grains

Oats, brown rice, and whole wheat provide energy and fiber.

Protein Foods

Eggs, beans, lentils, nuts, and yogurt help maintain muscles.
