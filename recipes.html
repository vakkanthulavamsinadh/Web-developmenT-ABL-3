Healthy Recipes

Vegetable Salad

Calories: Approximately 180 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Carrot
  • Lemon juice

Preparation: Chop the vegetables, add lemon juice, and mix well.

Fruit Oatmeal

Calories: Approximately 300 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Half cup oats
  • Milk
  • One banana
  • Almonds

Preparation: Cook oats in milk and add banana slices and almonds.

Lentil Soup

Calories: Approximately 230 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Half cup lentils
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Turmeric and salt

Preparation: Cook lentils with vegetables and spices until soft.
