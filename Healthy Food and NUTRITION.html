Welcome to Healthy Living

Discover healthy recipes, nutritious foods, and balanced meal plans for a healthier lifestyle.

Food Categories

Fruits

Apples, bananas, oranges, and berries provide vitamins and fiber.

Vegetables

Spinach, carrots, broccoli, and tomatoes provide essential nutrients.

Whole Grains

Oats, brown rice, and whole wheat provide energy and fiber.

Protein Foods

Eggs, beans, lentils, nuts, and yogurt help maintain muscles.

Healthy Recipes

Vegetable Salad

Calories: Approximately 180 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Cucumber
  • Tomato
  • Carrot
  • Lemon juice

Preparation: Chop the vegetables, add lemon juice, and mix well.

Fruit Oatmeal

Calories: Approximately 300 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Half cup oats
  • Milk
  • One banana
  • Almonds

Preparation: Cook oats in milk and add banana slices and almonds.

Lentil Soup

Calories: Approximately 230 kcal

Ingredients:

  • Half cup lentils
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Turmeric and salt

Preparation: Cook lentils with vegetables and spices until soft.

Daily Healthy Meal Plan

Breakfast

Oatmeal, banana, and milk.

Lunch

Brown rice, dal, and vegetable salad.

Evening Snack

Fresh fruit and a handful of nuts.

Dinner

Vegetable soup, chapati, and cooked vegetables.

Healthy Eating Tips

  • Eat a variety of fruits and vegetables.
  • Drink enough water throughout the day.
  • Choose whole grains and protein-rich foods.
  • Limit excess sugar, salt, and highly processed foods.
  • Eat balanced meals and maintain regular meal times.
  • Include physical activity in your daily routine.
